About Smart Placement & Career Portal

Connecting talented students with top companies through a modern and efficient placement platform.

Who We Are

Smart Placement & Career Portal is a web application developed to simplify the campus placement process. It enables students, companies, and administrators to collaborate through a single platform.

Students can create profiles, upload resumes, search for jobs, and apply online. Companies can post job opportunities, review applications, and shortlist candidates. Administrators can manage the complete placement process efficiently.

Our Mission

To provide students with equal access to placement opportunities while helping recruiters discover talented candidates quickly and efficiently.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted digital placement platform that bridges the gap between educational institutions and industry.
